Hacktoberfest, the annual open source holiday from DigitalOcean and amazing sponsors, has a very warm place in my heart. At it's core, [Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.digitalocean.com/ "Hacktoberfest") is about drawing attention to the open source community, helping people maintain their projects, teaching people how to code, and encouraging people to learn new skills such as Git and GitHub.

Every October is now my Hacktober and I eagerly await the glorious overlap of pumpkin spice and pull requests.
